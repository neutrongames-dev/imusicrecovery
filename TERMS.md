# iMusicRecovery Terms of Use — draft

This repository includes draft product terms for development convenience. They are not a substitute for legal review before public distribution.

The application is provided on an as-is and as-available basis to the extent permitted by applicable law. Recovery is not guaranteed. Aging storage, hardware failure, filesystem corruption, USB interruptions, operating-system or driver failures, and software defects may prevent recovery or produce incomplete results.

iMusicRecovery is designed for read-only media recovery and does not intentionally erase, restore, sync, or modify media on the connected iPod. Users should keep the original device unchanged until they independently confirm that recovered files are complete and usable.
